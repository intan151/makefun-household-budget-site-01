HomeLedger — a household budget planner that runs entirely in your browser. Your figures never leave this device; they are stored only in your browser's local storage.

Sample household figures are included so every page works out of the box — edit them on the Budget page or add your own expenses and they update everywhere. Built as a static site: no accounts, no tracking, no network requests.
